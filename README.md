Word of Mouth

A community-driven app helping people experiencing homelessness or food insecurity find resources they can actually access.

Word of Mouth combines verified information with real-world community reports about food, shelter, clothing, showers, healthcare, housing, transportation, and other essential services.

The initial focus is North Bay, Ontario, with the goal of expanding to communities across Canada.

Goal

Make it easy for someone to answer one simple question:

"Where can I actually get help right now?"

Project Status

Early development — MVP.
